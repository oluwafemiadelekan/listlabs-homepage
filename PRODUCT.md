## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js 16 (App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind v4), Framer Motion (`motion` package), Phosphor Icons, Lenis smooth scroll. Chosen by Femi. Lives in `site/`.

## Users

Primary: people who move things through relationships and already carry a buyer list in their head or phone. Collectibles dealers, classic and specialty car dealers, small-business brokers. The brand guide also names real estate pros and investors. Situation: new stock or a new mandate arrives and they need the right few people to know, without telling everyone.

## Product Purpose

You list what you have and choose who sees the listing. To look for something, you set a saved search that watches for it. Matching starts with people you already know. Success for the homepage is a visitor understanding this in seconds and claiming a handle. Handle claims are the only metric.

## Positioning

Five visibility levels per listing (Private, Unlisted, Shared, Listed, Public) give a private middle ground that public marketplaces and group chats cannot offer. Not a want-ad board, not one big public catalog, no cut taken. "Built for closing, not posting."

## Operating Context

Deals close off-platform through relationships. The product replaces copy-paste outreach, forwarded one-pagers and public auctions. Looking is never a listing of a want; it is a standing search.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Listings with photos and documents, editable after publishing.
- Visibility per listing: Private (people you invite), Unlisted (people with the link), Shared (members who follow you), Listed (members you don't know), Public (everyone, on every integrated platform).
- Promotion by link anywhere, and cross-promotion by trusted members who opt in.
- Public profiles at a handle, a feed scoped to what you have access to, embeddable widgets and sites.
- Undecided (client): whether "Claim your handle" creates an account or joins a waitlist; nav button wording; final section order; final company name and logo.
- Terminology: listing, handle, saved search, visibility level, cross-promotion. Never "page", never "marketplace" as a headline, never want-ads.

## Brand Commitments

Working name ListLabs with a provisional mark, both built to swap in one pass. Inter for all type. The client's colour is violet (#8B5CF6 in their guide); this design keeps violet as a family, plum, lavender and a muted violet mark, chosen by Femi (see DESIGN.md). Thin low-opacity borders, soft shadows, no gradients or glows. Voice: lead with the reader's pain, then the fix; conversational and senior; no buzzwords; no em dashes. Homepage copy is fixed word for word by the client. Reference feel: Linear, Stripe, Vercel. Femi's visual references: see reference/design-references.md.

## Product Principles

1. One action per page: claim a handle. Everything else explains or proves.
2. The visibility levels are the product's idea; show them, do not describe them.
3. Private first: the page should feel like a tool for quiet dealmaking, not a feed.
4. Real copy only, and only the client's copy.
5. Keep the name and mark swappable.
