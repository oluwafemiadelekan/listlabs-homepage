01

Foundation

Unchanged from the client. Everything else serves these lines.

Mission

Open access to opportunity.

What it is

You list what you have, and you choose who sees it. To look for something, you set a saved search that watches for it. Matching starts with people you already know.

What it is not

Not a want-ad board. Not one big public catalog. Not a marketplace taking a cut.

Who visits

People who move things through relationships: collectibles dealers, classic and specialty car dealers, small-business brokers. The homepage stays general and never names them in the hero.

The one action

Claim a handle. It is the only thing the homepage measures.

Statement with highlight

You get a listing, and you choose who sees it. Looking is a saved search that watches.

One phrase per statement gets the outlined pill. At most one small filled mark. Both are muted violet.

Steps

The section note sits directly under "How it works". The active row's lines thicken and a soft highlight travels along them, left to right.

  1. 01List itDescribe what you have. Photos, docs, whatever helps.
  2. 02Decide who sees itPrivate, Unlisted, Shared, Listed, Public.
  3. 03Promote itDrop the link anywhere you already talk.

The active row gets the filled number and full ink. On the site a rounded tile floats beside it and glides between rows on a spring. Step two's tile is the visibility field: you at the centre, people lighting outward from Private to Public.

07

Imagery

Real objects people list, never stock dashboards. Photos are cut into rounded squares and wide rectangles, 4px apart.

The grid

14 columns, 3 rows, filling the hero. Rows 1 and 2: three photos cut into squares and wide rectangles. Row 3: five products with price chips, aligned to the columns above. No image appears twice on the page.

The cut

Each photo stays one image. A page-coloured ring around each cell draws the gaps, so nothing is distorted.

Words go in blocks

Text never sits on a photo and there are no fades. The words get their own white block 4px below. Stand-in photos are credited in site/public/images/CREDITS.md.

09

Voice

From the client, unchanged. Homepage copy is fixed word for word.

How it sounds

  • Lead with the reader's pain, then the fix.
  • Conversational and senior.
  • Say the plain thing. No buzzwords.
  • No em dashes. Commas, full stops or parentheses.

Calls to action

  • Primary: "Claim your handle".
  • Listing starter: "Start listing".
  • Helper: "Free to join. First in, first found."

Never on the homepage

  • "Turn your network into deals" as the H1.
  • "Not another marketplace" as a headline.
  • Calling ListLabs "a page".
  • Wants listed like want-ads.
  • Named example people. Compliance or AI as features.
10

Do and don't

The short version. If a change breaks one of these, it needs a reason.

Do

  • Keep tiles 4px apart and radii large.
  • Use plum for weight, lavender for softness.
  • Keep muted violet to marks: the @, the highlight, the dot.
  • Make every arrow a real link.
  • Animate every state change, including anchor jumps.
  • Keep the name and mark in their single files.

Don't

  • Fill a block with bright violet.
  • Add decorative arrows, icon circles or play buttons.
  • Add eyebrows, tags or labels the client did not write.
  • Use gradients or glows as decoration, or put text over photos.
  • Change the layout when the task is colour.
  • Use ease-in, or let anything snap.
