The best deals move through relationships, not public marketplaces.

02 / Feel

Brand feel and art direction

Premium, modern Silicon Valley. Confident and quiet. If a choice adds noise, cut it.

Principles

  • Restraint over decoration. Generous whitespace. Let elements breathe.
  • High craft: crisp type, precise alignment, one consistent 8px spacing scale.
  • Premium and modern, not flashy. Reference points: Linear, Stripe, Vercel.
  • Clean surfaces, subtle 1px low-opacity borders, soft shadows. No heavy gradients, no busy textures, no glows.
  • Violet used sparingly, as accent and for primary actions, never as large flat fills.
  • Dark, confident hero. Calm, light content sections.
  • Motion subtle and fast (150-200ms ease-out), for clarity only.
✓ Do

One accent color. Lots of space. Sharp type. Quiet 1px borders and soft shadows that you barely notice.

× Avoid

Gradients everywhere. Glows. Drop shadows on everything. Cramped layouts and busy textures.

Color system

Violet leads. Charcoal carries the words. Neutrals hold the room. Click any swatch to copy its hex.

Primary: violet

Violet ramp

Functional states

Functional UI states only, for system feedback (success, warning, error/delete). The brand palette stays violet plus charcoal. No blue.

Usage: violet is for primary actions and accents, not large flood fills. Charcoal (ink) is for text. Keep backgrounds neutral (white and grays) so the violet stays loud. 08 / Voice (most important)

Voice and tone

Lead with the reader's pain, then the fix. Talk like a sharp person who respects the reader's time.

Principles

  • Lead with the reader's pain, then the fix.
  • Conversational and senior. Let the reader breathe.
  • No em dashes. Use commas, periods, or parentheses (per the Go-Live ticket voice guardrails: "no buzzwords, no em dashes, conversational").
  • No buzzwords. Say the plain thing.

Calls to action

Primary CTA is always "Claim your handle." Secondary is "Sign up free."

Taglines

Primary tagline:

Turn your network into deals.

Rotation (use when you need variety):

  • Say it once. Reach the right buyer.
  • Your buyer list, finally out of your head.
  • Stop posting. Start closing.

Dos and don'ts

Same message, on-brand voice. Lead with the reader's pain, then the fix.

Don't write thisWrite this instead
Leverage our ecosystem to synergize with stakeholders. Reach the right buyer without the noise.
Utilize the platform to spin up your network presence. Get your listings in front of people who actually buy.
A next-generation solution empowering users to unlock value. Your buyer list, finally out of your head.
Reach out to circle back and hedge against churn. Stop posting. Start closing.

Buzzwords to avoid (examples)

The documented rule is simply: no buzzwords. These are common offenders.

ListLab is a platform for independent dealers, brokers, and investors who move assets through private relationships. It replaces copy-paste outreach and public listings with structured listings, standing searches, and private matching, so members reach the right buyer or seller directly and close more of the deals already in their network.

09 / Motion

Motion

Subtle and fast. Motion supports clarity, never decoration.

  • Duration: 150ms to 200ms.
  • Easing: ease-out.
  • Move a little, not a lot (a few pixels, a soft fade).
  • Respect reduced-motion preferences.
