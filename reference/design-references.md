# Design references

The images themselves are other designers' work and are not included in this repo. This file records what was taken from each.

## Layout: a photographer's landing page (Russian-language concept, "Один фотограф. Сотни важных историй.")

Followed closely for structure.

- Quiet header: name left, small text links right, one dark pill button with an arrow circle.
- Hero row: a two-line headline on the left, a short line and the action on the right.
- A wide photo strip cut into rounded squares and wide rectangles, a few pixels apart.
- A centred statement paragraph with one phrase set in an outlined pill and one small inline icon.
- Bento: one wide neutral tile holding a heading, beside a 2x2 block of coloured tiles.
- Gallery: a dark title tile set into a grid of photos of different heights.
- Numbered steps: pill numbers, titles, descriptions on the right, and a photo that floats beside the active row.

## Colour: BloomFi, a stablecoin landing page (by @kate_koma)

Followed for palette only.

- A pale cool grey page.
- Deep plum for dark cards and buttons.
- Lavender for light cards, sometimes with a faint wash.
- Violet appears in small amounts, never as a loud block.
